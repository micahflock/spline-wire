Inside: the chain wrapped around it. Outside: the chain pressed into a hollow. After the photo is measured you can tap the exact edge on it.
For a good measurement
  • Lay the chain flat on a plain surface, dots facing up.
  • Get the whole chain in the frame and fill most of it.
  • Shoot at 1× (no zoom). A slight angle is fine.
  • Avoid glare and hard shadows on the chain.
