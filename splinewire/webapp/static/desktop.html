Settings

Camera
iPhone focal length, mm (35 mm equiv.) Use this focal length for every photo

Photos sent from an iPhone's browser lose their camera data, so the app uses the first number. Main (1×) camera: 24 mm on iPhone 14 Pro, iPhone 15 and later; 26 mm on earlier models. Shoot at 1×.

Chain
Fill in for a chain Link length, pin to pin (mm) What touches the object Pin to link edge, or sleeve radius (mm) Mark on each pin Mark diameter (mm) Ring hole diameter (mm) Sharpest bend at a joint (°)

Change these only for a different chain; "Fill in for a chain" sets them for a known one. The O-ring chain bends up to 125°, the snap-fit chain 110°, the print-in-place chain 80°. The pin count and the object's side are set for each photo.
