Putting one joint together

  1. Cut the thread. Drive a screw into the outer link's sleeve until its head touches the sleeve's end, then back it out. The sleeve's hole is {{PILOT}} mm, so the M3 cuts its own thread.
  2. Outer link dots down. The sleeve points up at you. Use the Dots down button to see it this way.
  3. O-ring on. Slide it over the {{SLEEVE}} mm sleeve until it sits in the 45° seat under the plate.
  4. Washer on. {{WASHER_ID}} mm hole over the {{SLEEVE}} mm sleeve; it rests on the O-ring.
  5. Inner link on. Its {{HOLE}} mm hole goes over the sleeve, raised ring toward the washer, countersink facing you.
  6. Screw in until it stops hard. It firms up as the O-ring squeezes, then stops when the head meets the sleeve's end. Stop there: that sets the squeeze.
  7. Check. No play when you push and pull, smooth friction, and a firm stop at about {{BETWEEN_ROUND}}° between the links.
