The first visit downloads about 40 MB, once. You can take the photo meanwhile.

The camera shows the chain it finds, says what to fix, and takes the photo once the chain has been still for a moment. The shutter button works any time.

Library photos keep the focal length. The system camera's photos lose it on iPhone and Pixel phones, so it then comes from Settings (your saved or learned focal length) or is estimated from the chain, which is less accurate. The live camera is off in this mode.

Tap a pin to remove it, a dot to add one. Drag to move, pinch to zoom.

            Learned from photos that carry it, never from the front camera. Android's in-page camera often leaves it out: teach it once from any photo this phone's main camera took.

            Leave it off unless a lens reports a wrong focal length.

            For a good photo
            • Lay the chain flat on a plain surface, dots facing up.
            • Get the whole chain in the frame and fill most of it.
            • Shoot at 1× (no zoom), straight down. The camera takes the photo once the chain has been still for a moment.
            • Avoid glare and hard shadows on the chain.
