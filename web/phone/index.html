    Photos

    On this phone

    An older photo is deleted and its curve kept: it can still be copied, downloaded and sent, but its pins can no longer be fixed. Photos never leave this phone; only the curve is sent.

    Debug

    The override is used for every photo, over its camera data: for a lens whose EXIF is off. Live view above also offers the chain only, and the camera shows a DEBUG key.

    For a good photo

    • Lay the chain flat on a plain surface, dots facing up.
    • Get the whole chain in the frame and fill most of it.
    • Shoot at 1× (no zoom), straight down. The camera takes the photo once the chain has been still for a moment.
    • Avoid glare and hard shadows on the chain.

    Tap a pin to remove it, a dot to add one. Drag to move, pinch to zoom, double-tap to fit.
