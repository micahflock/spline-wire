Field of view: stream vs still

  1. Prop the phone so it does not move (lean it on a cup or a stack of books), pointed at something with clear detail near all four edges, e.g. a page of text or a ruler.
  2. Start the camera, then tap Grab video frame.
  3. Without moving the phone, take the still: Still via takePhoto, or if that is not available, A. Camera above and then Use latest photo.
  4. In the overlay, drag Frame size until the red-outlined video frame lines up with the still (use Frame opacity to compare), then tap Record match. 100% means the stream sees as much as the still; 80% means the stream is a crop of the still's centre.
