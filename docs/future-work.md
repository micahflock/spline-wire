## Website and engine

- **WebGPU detection.** Move the detector's pixel work to the GPU where it measurably helps (D18's order of work). Today a 12 MP photo measures in 1.1 s in Chromium and detection took 2.2 s on an iPhone before the faster detector, so this waits for a phone that shows the need.
  - *Looked at 2026-11-18 for the live camera view: not now.* The live view already analyses ~16 frames a second (the coach needs a few); its limit was resolution, which CPU work fixed (2026-11-19, [experiments/live_view/README.md](../experiments/live_view/README.md), Findings: a one-pass threshold, regions round the tracked chain, the grab in a worker; the detector now costs 1–2 ms a tracked frame and 7–9 ms scanning). Only the local threshold suits a GPU (~60% of the live detector's time); the connected components (~38%) are serial, and readback adds a frame or two of latency. For the still, the GPU could save perhaps 0.2–0.5 s of 1–2 s, at the cost of a second implementation to keep in line with the golden files. WebGPU is in Safari on iOS 26+ and Chrome on Android (Samsung Xclipse GPUs uncertain), not in Firefox on Android, so a CPU fallback stays. If a GPU is ever needed for the live view, WebGL2 (a gray + threshold shader, async R8 readback) reaches every tested phone; the trigger is a phone's "frame X+Y ms" over ~30 ms with the regions (X is the grab, now in a worker where the browser can). Figures: `experiments/live_view/`.
- **Core memory.** The TypeScript detector peaks about 90 MB above the loaded 12 MP photo (Node, peak RSS), freed afterwards. What is left is the full-size float32 copy of the photo for refinement (48 MB) and the Int32 label image (48 MB). Work on it only if a phone needs it.

- **HELL MODE, a hidden third theme, as in the owner's Sketchworx** (micahflock/sketchworx, its `PROGRESS.md`: "Reskin" and "HELL MODE colors"). There, setting any dimension to 666 unlocks it the first time (saved in the browser) and switches to it. After that, the theme button cycles light → dark → HELL MODE, and the command box lists it. Everything is `#000` and one accent (`#FF1F1F` by default; a swatch button offers eight: red, green, pink, orange, yellow, blue, purple, white). With one colour, state is shown by line shape instead of hue: dashed, solid, zigzag with ✕ marks, a blinking readout (struck through with reduced motion). The active key is shown by inverting it, and labels are bare heavy glyphs in a pixel font (Silkscreen). Here, the pages only follow the system's light or dark (`phone.css`, `desktop/style.css`), with no toggle, so this needs a theme button first. It also needs a hidden trigger (666 typed in a number field, say a pin count or a pitch), and a one-colour form of everything the pages now say by colour: the ok/warn/bad lamps, the coach's message, and the live overlay's traced pins and ring. The live camera view must stay readable over a photo.

## Chain

The owner's plans for the snap-fit chain (2026-11-20); none designed yet.

- **Dual-colour printing.** Print the links and their fiducial rims in two colours on a multi-material printer instead of one colour or a filament swap, so the rims' contrast with the links is part of the print. Pick the pair for detection: detection reads gray, so the two colours must differ clearly in gray, not just in hue (orange rims on off-white links fail on dark tables; see *Fiducial colours picked from a photo* below). Check a pair on the simulated tables before printing it. *2026-11-28: with a colour channel chosen in Settings (web-build.md work queue item 13), a pair need only differ clearly in one of red, green, blue or gray.*
- **A chain that folds up neatly.** A chain that packs into a compact, tidy bundle for carrying and storage, without tangling or losing the pose-holding grip of its joints.
- **A QR code on one side, visible when folded.** The fiducials are on one face only; put a printed QR code on the other face, placed so it shows on the outside of the folded chain. It could carry the chain's settings (preset, pitch, contact, Colours), so the phone page sets itself up from a scan rather than the person choosing them in Settings, or link to the website. Open: how a QR code prints legibly at link size on a 0.4 mm nozzle, and whether it must be split across links that only line up when folded. *2026-11-28: a printed card's QR code with the chain's calibration is planned (web-build.md work queue item 14, O12); a code on the chain itself would carry the same link.*

## Measurement

- **Sharp corners between short walls.** A corner marked on the drawing is rebuilt from the walls either side (`web/src/desktop/corners.ts`, D27 in web-build.md), each read from two pins resting on it, so steps shorter than about three pitches (40 mm with the snap-fit chain) come out wrong by several mm (corners.test.ts, the log of 2026-11-20). A global fit could do better: a polygon with a vertex at every marked corner, fitted so that each pin's sleeve or link arm touches it and none cuts into it, which also uses the pins lifted over a ridge and the ones touching the tip.

- **Fiducial colours picked from a photo.** Detection reads a gray image (Pillow's luma, and the live view's like it), so a chain is found only when its rims are clearly lighter or darker in gray than both the links and the table. Coloured rims fail that on some tables: orange rims (gray ≈ 148) on off-white links (≈ 230), in a friend's Firefox-on-Android screenshot (2026-11-19, build 19) on a dark cutting mat. Modelled at span 0.30 with Colours: dark, the live view found 0 of 13 on a dark table (≈ 60), 9 of 13 tracked (0 from a cold start) on mid-gray (≈ 120), and 13 of 13 on light gray or white paper; black rims were found on all four. The idea: the person taps a rim and a link in a photo, and the page picks the colour channel or weighting that separates them most (for orange on off-white, blue: orange is then about as dark as black), saved with the chain's settings. It changes how the measuring pipeline turns colour into gray, in `splinewire/detect.py` and the TypeScript port alike (`decode.ts` and the live view's `toGray`), with the golden files regenerated. Until then: black rims, or Colours: dark and a light surface under the chain. *2026-11-28: the manual step, a channel chosen in Settings, is planned as web-build.md work queue item 13; picking it from two taps stays here.*
