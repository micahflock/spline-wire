What changed from today

  • Pins live in the header, with a drawing of the chain that grows and wraps.
  • Scan and Upload are two keys. The mode switch is gone.
  • The working curve is one card. Edits, numbers and warnings live in its expanded view.
  • Past curves are a grid of drawings, no photos. Tap one to make it the working curve.
  • Curves send themselves to the paired computer, or queue until it's back. Copy, Share and the files moved to Settings.
  • "Tap its side" is gone. Swap side flips the curve.
  • The camera has no shutter or Upload key: it takes the photo once the chain holds still (the orange bar fills). Diagnostics hide behind Debug.
  • Expand and collapse share the card's bottom-right corner. Double-tap the photo to fit.

About the pictures

The photos are the app's own synthetic test photos of the snap-fit chain, measured by the real pipeline, so the curves and pins are true to the code. The pin markers follow the unmerged overlay branch (outlines, no numbers).
